# COBALT SKIES 2026 — LOCKED MASTER BRAND GUIDELINES

**Institution:** Bangalore Medical College and Research Institute (BMCRI), Bengaluru.
**Identity status:** Creative identity and collateral system; institutional approval pending.

## 1. Source of truth
`assets/CS_2026_ORIGINAL_LOCKED.png` is the **exact original uploaded 1254 × 1254 RGBA image**. Do not redraw, vectorize, recolour, crop, retouch, distort, or substitute it. The black field is part of the supplied master. All templates reproduce the complete original as a rectangular/square image. A transparent cutout (`assets/transparent/`) is provided for placing the emblem on other dark or light backgrounds; the inside of the ring stays solid.

## 2. Symbolism
Phoenix (C) = Agni/fire and Vayu/air. Serpent (S) = Jala/water. Mountains = Prithvi/earth. Celestial star and rings = Aakasha/space.

## 3. Hierarchy
1. Locked emblem 2. COBALT SKIES 3. 2026 4. BMCRI/annual cultural fest 5. campaign information.

## 4. Typography
Preferred fonts: Cinzel (display), Montserrat (functional). Web fallback: Georgia and system sans. These are recommendations; they are not embedded proprietary fonts. Keep letter spacing moderate and avoid decorative type for schedules and long copy.

## 5. Logo use
Maintain clear space of at least 10% of emblem width. Do not place text or marks over the emblem. For small formats use the complete emblem only where details remain legible; otherwise use the festival wordmark without falsely claiming a simplified emblem. On black, use the master as-is. On any other background, use the transparent cutout rather than a black square.

## 6. Applications
The templates folder contains campaign-ready visual **starting points** for Instagram, stage LED, merchandise artwork, banners and certificates. They are not photographed product mockups, nor machine-ready print/embroidery files. Event dates, artists, sponsors, venues and prices are intentionally omitted.

## 7. Production and approvals
RGB PNGs are suitable for digital mockups. For commercial printing, have a printer create a properly profiled CMYK conversion and approve proofs. Pantone matches, embroidery stitch files, physical stage measurements, editable layered artwork, and final approvals are not included. The master is raster. The SVGs in `assets/vector/` either embed the raster or are single-colour silhouette proofs; a full-colour vector redraw is not supplied.

## 8. Credit and crest
Use the full institutional name correctly. Do not invent or substitute an official BMCRI crest. Add only an approved crest supplied by BMCRI.
