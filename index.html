06 / Production standards

Keep the identity consistent

Always

Use the exact original square master.
Keep the full emblem visible.
Use cobalt, midnight navy, gold and silver as primary brand colours.
Maintain readable type and contrast.
Use accurate BMCRI attribution.

Never

Recreate, vectorize or replace the CS emblem.
Remove the black background and claim it is official transparency.
Overlay event copy on the emblem.
Use unapproved institutional crests.
Claim print profiling or embroidery digitization is completed.
